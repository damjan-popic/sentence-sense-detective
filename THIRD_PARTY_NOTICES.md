## Open American National Corpus

The diversity cap on MASC source documents makes a written OANC fallback necessary. The fallback is selected only from the official OANC GrAF archive.

- Official source: https://anc.org/data/oanc/download/
- Archive used: https://www.anc.org/OANC/OANC_GrAF.zip
- Archive SHA-256: `5a26559a1becba41a527cb674fff4fb9c4fb70b276f60422c4f07a0ef23fd867`
- Terms: unrestricted use and redistribution, including research, development, and commercial development, as stated on the official OANC download page
- Attribution: Open American National Corpus

Only the selected 10,000-sentence derivative and its provenance are part of the project data. The original archives and full extracted source data remain in the ignored local cache.

About Sentence Sense Detective

Small rounds, useful feedback.

Sentence Sense Detective is a lightweight classroom companion for practising parts of speech, sentence elements, and clause analysis.

  1. The target is highlighted for you.
  2. A correct first answer earns one point.
  3. After a mistake, you get one learning retry.
  4. At the end, you can review everything you missed.

The English pilot uses carefully checked examples and explanations. The same practice format can later support other languages.

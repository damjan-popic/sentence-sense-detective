Sources and licences

The expanded English practice bank is built from openly available American English corpus material. Each selected sentence keeps its source, collection, document, licence, and attribution in the project data.

MASC 3.0.0

The primary source is the Manually Annotated Sub-Corpus, published by the Open American National Corpus project. The selected derivative is attributed to MASC and is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States licence.

Official MASC information · Official download page

Written Open American National Corpus

The written OANC supplies the documented fallback needed to meet the project’s source-diversity limit. The official OANC download page permits use and redistribution for research and development, including commercial development.

Official OANC information · Official download page

What is published

The practice site loads only the question files it needs for a round. Original source archives remain outside the website. The versioned 10,000-sentence derivative, provenance, and preparation reports are maintained with the project and published through GitHub Releases.
